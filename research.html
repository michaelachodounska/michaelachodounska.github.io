research

Here, you can see a snippet of my most relevant work (so far)!

Since August 2026, I have been working with Maribel Romero (University of Konstanz) on expletive negation in the SFB 1760 Silence, Noise and Signal in Language.

Between 2024 and 2026, I studied for my Master’s degree at the University of Potsdam, where I had the chance to meet and work with amazing people. So, in this snippet, you will see work on comparatives that came out of Anna Struck’s seminar, fieldwork research on particles in Asante Twi that was influenced by Jeanne Lecavelier and Malte Zimmermann, and my master’s thesis written under the supervision of Masha Privizentseva on negation in Czech polar questions.

Since 2025, I have been working on the politeness of Czech indirect requests in collaboration with Radim Lacina (Masaryk University). In this (side) project, we’re looking at how negation in these requests (uttered as polar questions) changes the perception of politeness.

In the past, I worked with Radek Šimík (Charles University) on epistemic particles in Czech polar questions. I looked at the pragmatics of the epistemic particle náhodou and its contribution to the meaning of both matrix and embedded interrogative clauses. Right now, I try to follow up on this research with fieldwork on polar questions in Asante Twi!
