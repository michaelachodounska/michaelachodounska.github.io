research

Here, you can see a small snippet of my most relevant work so far!

Currently, I’m working on the politeness of Czech indirect requests in collaboration with Radim Lacina. In this (side) project, we’re looking at how negation in these requests (uttered as polar questions) changes the perception of politeness.

In the past, I worked with Radek Šimík on epistemic particles in Czech polar questions. I specifically looked at the pragmatics of the epistemic particle náhodou and its contribution to the meaning of both matrix and embedded interrogative clauses.
